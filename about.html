About us

The best AI tools feel like a great colleague, not a search box.

KingxTech started as a small team frustrated by how much friction sat between a good idea and a working prototype. Today we build it from Kaduna, Nigeria.

Mission

Give every developer an AI that reasons with them, not just for them — from a first question to a shipped, hosted app.

Vision

A world where the gap between an idea and working software is a conversation, not a quarter of engineering time.

How we work

Small team, fast iteration, and products we use ourselves every day. If it is not good enough for our workflow, it is not shipping.
