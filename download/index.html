NeuroCore

Download NeuroCore for Android

NeuroCore is a private AI assistant that runs on your phone and keeps working without an internet connection.

Download for Android (APK)Read the announcement

Android only for now. The download is an APK file, installed directly rather than through an app store. If the button does not start the download, open it in Google Drive and tap Download.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android and wait for the file to finish downloading.
  2. Open the downloaded NeuroCore.apk from your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager for this one time.
  4. Tap Install, then open NeuroCore. On first launch it shows “Preparing your private AI” while it gets ready.
