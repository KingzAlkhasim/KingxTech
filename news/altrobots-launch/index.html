A new product from KingxTech

Your AI workforce.
Always at work.

Meet AltroBots — an Android AI assistant being built to help you coordinate capable AI agents, connect the tools you already use, and keep meaningful work moving while you focus on what matters.

Download for AndroidBack to newsroom
Available now · Android v1.0.0
The idea

Less busywork. More intentional work.

AltroBots is being developed around a simple idea: your AI should do more than answer a prompt. It should help organise tasks, work with your connected services and keep you informed when your attention is needed.

✦

Coordinate AI agents

Bring agent-driven tasks into one place and make it easier to see what is running, what has finished and what needs attention.

⌘

Connect your workflow

The product is being designed to work with services such as GitHub, X and WhatsApp, alongside AI providers you choose and configure.

◉

Stay in control

Approval prompts are part of the vision: when an action needs your decision, you should be able to review it instead of letting an agent guess.

⌁

Built for Android

AltroBots is being developed as an Android application, with a mobile-first experience for checking in on your AI workforce.
