# KingxTech News publishing guide

## News vs Blog
- **News** is the official company newsroom: launches, partnerships, events, milestones, company statements and major product updates.
- **Blog** is for engineering articles, tutorials, architecture, development logs and technical lessons.

## Publishing a news article
1. Create a directory such as `news/k-xpertai-launch/` and put the article in `index.html`. This produces the clean URL `/news/k-xpertai-launch/` on GitHub Pages.
2. Use the newsroom page's shared navigation, footer and `/assets/site.css` styles.
3. Include a unique title, summary, canonical URL, publication date, category and Open Graph metadata.
4. Add a card linking to the article in `news.html`, and add its URL to `sitemap.xml`.
5. Publish only confirmed company facts. Keep draft/announced/in-development statuses precise; never imply a partnership, launch or milestone is complete before it is official.

Add new cards to the grid in `news.html`, newest first.
