KingxTech partners with Kitoko Hub to improve tech learning in Nigeria

Learning the basics of tech is easier than ever. Knowing how to build something real with them is the hard part. KingxTech and Kitoko Hub are teaming up to close that gap, starting with a hands-on workshop for the next generation of tech creators.

From the basics to building

Across Nigeria, a young generation is curious about AI and eager to build with it. Many of them have watched the tutorials, learned the vocabulary and finished the introductory courses. The next step is the one that matters: turning that knowledge into working projects that other people can use.

That is the idea behind the AI & Tech Creators Workshop, which Kitoko Hub is running under the banner “Go Beyond The Basics”. KingxTech is joining as a partner, providing technical support, tools, mentorship and teaching, to make it a practical, build-first experience: learn to build, deploy and innovate with AI.

Why KingxTech and Kitoko Hub

KingxTech is a Nigerian-built platform made in Kaduna for building, hosting and shipping web apps with an AI that works inside your project. Kitoko Hub brings a community of learners and a clear focus on tech education. Together, the partnership connects people who want to learn with the tools and thinking used to build products for real users.

What KingxTech brings

KingxTech is not only putting its name on the workshop. It is taking part in the learning itself:

  • Technical support: help when participants get stuck, so a small error does not end a good idea.
  • Tools: access to KingxTech's tools, so learners practise on the same kind of platform used to build and ship real products.
  • Mentorship: guidance from builders who work with these tools every day.
  • Teaching: hands-on lessons that show how to go from an idea to a working project.

The aim is for participants to learn inside the KingxTech ecosystem: build, test and publish in one place, instead of juggling a different tool for every step. When learning and building happen together, skills stick, and a finished project is something you can show others.

What the workshop covers

  • AI Engineering: building applications powered by AI.
  • Machine Learning: understanding how models learn and how to put them to work.
  • DevOps: taking what you build and shipping it so it runs reliably.

Those three tracks follow a project from idea to launch: build with AI, understand the models behind it, and deploy it for the world to use.

Key details

  • Starts: February 2027
  • Format: online, on Google Classroom, so you can join from anywhere in Nigeria
  • Registration fee: 20k, as listed by Kitoko Hub
  • Follow Kitoko Hub: @kitoko_hub
