Platform update · November 7, 2026

Build your backend at KingxTech

KingxTech now offers backend support for users. You can build the backend of your app on KingxTech, in the same place where you build and publish your frontend.

What this means for you

Until now, KingxTech has been about building, hosting and shipping the front of your web app. With backend support, the server side of your project can live with it too, so you no longer need to stitch a separate service together before your app can do real work.

Get started

Create a free account, open a project and start building. Documentation for backend features is available in the KingxTech docs, and you can send feedback through our contact page or on Discord.

Follow KingxTech for updates and announcements as they happen.
